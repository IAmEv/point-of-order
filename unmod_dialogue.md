# Phase 3 Caucus Dialogue — Draft Scripts (v2)

**8 scripts total** (4 topic buckets × 2 leader variants). ~12 turns each.
Voice: high-school-simple. No MUN jargon (no "bloc position," "preamble," "operative clause," "working paper"). Delegates say "we." Conversations are about actual ideas, not paper structure.
Only Script 1 uses the "Power already wrote it alone" framing.
Placeholders: `{country}` = speaker's country, `{boss}` = Dem-Boy's paired Power-Del country, `{demboyCountry}` = the bloc's Dem-Boy's country.
Slacker never speaks. Hold silent when the script ends. No em-dashes.

Bucket mapping (unchanged):
- **substance** = funding mechanisms, enforcement measures, sanctions, monitoring frameworks
- **process** = implementation timeline, definitions, next steps, the working paper
- **people** = humanitarian concerns, regional cooperation, root causes
- **opening** = opening statements, bloc positions

---

## 1. SUBSTANCE, Power-led  *(the one script framed as "Power already wrote it")*

1. **Power:** "So {country} already wrote up what we should do. Every country pays two percent of their GDP, no exceptions."
2. **Dem-Boy:** "Yeah, {boss} showed me the paper right before this. It is really solid."
3. **Carolina:** "Wait, {country} was thinking maybe smaller countries could pay less,"
4. **Power:** "No. Two percent for everyone. If we start giving exceptions the whole thing falls apart."
5. **Dem-Boy:** "{boss} is right, exceptions kill the whole point."
6. **Carolina:** "But if some countries literally cannot afford it they will just say no,"
7. **Power:** "Then they say no. We are not going to weaken this because some people cannot keep up."
8. **Dem-Boy:** "Exactly. If we lower the number, {boss}'s whole plan gets watered down."
9. **Carolina:** "Could we at least add something about helping countries that struggle to pay,"
10. **Power:** "That is a separate conversation. Right now we are locking in the two percent."
11. **Dem-Boy:** "{boss} already has the wording done. We just need everyone to sign on."
12. **Power:** "Send it around. We are done debating this."

## 2. SUBSTANCE, Carolina-led

1. **Carolina:** "Okay, before we write anything down, {country} wants to hear what everyone thinks the GDP contribution should be."
2. **Power:** "Easy. Two percent across the board. That is fair."
3. **Carolina:** "That is one idea. {demboyCountry}, you were saying something earlier?"
4. **Dem-Boy:** "Uh, yeah. {country} was thinking maybe it should depend on how much money a country actually has..."
5. **Power:** "That is way too complicated. Just pick a number and move on."
6. **Carolina:** "Hold on, let {demboyCountry} finish. That could actually work."
7. **Dem-Boy:** "So like, richer countries pay more, poorer countries pay less. Same total in the end."
8. **Power:** "That is going to take forever to figure out. Two percent is simple."
9. **Carolina:** "It is a little more work but it is way more fair. {demboyCountry} is right."
10. **Power:** "Fine, but there has to be a floor. No one pays less than half a percent."
11. **Carolina:** "Deal. {demboyCountry}, can you write up the sliding scale while {boss} sets the floor?"
12. **Dem-Boy:** "Yeah, {country} can do that. Give me a minute."

## 3. PROCESS, Power-led

1. **Power:** "Alright, here's how it goes. Implementation starts in six months, everyone has to be on board within a year."
2. **Dem-Boy:** "{boss}'s timeline makes sense. Fast but doable."
3. **Carolina:** "Six months feels really fast, {country} was thinking maybe a year to start,"
4. **Power:** "A year is too slow. Half the countries will lose interest by then."
5. **Dem-Boy:** "Yeah, if we wait too long people just move on."
6. **Carolina:** "But some smaller countries need time to actually set things up on their end..."
7. **Power:** "That is their problem. We set the deadline, they figure it out."
8. **Dem-Boy:** "Exactly what {boss} said. If we let people stall nothing happens."
9. **Carolina:** "What about a check-in halfway through, just to see where everyone is..."
10. **Power:** "No check-ins. Check-ins are how people push back the real deadline."
11. **Dem-Boy:** "{boss} is right, check-ins turn into extensions."
12. **Power:** "Six months to start, one year to be done. Everyone gets the same clock."

## 4. PROCESS, Carolina-led

1. **Carolina:** "So we need to figure out when this idea actually happens. What are people thinking?"
2. **Power:** "Six months to start. One year to finish. Simple."
3. **Carolina:** "That is fast. {demboyCountry}, you had a different idea, right?"
4. **Dem-Boy:** "Um, yeah. {country} was thinking we should do it in steps, like the easy stuff first..."
5. **Power:** "Steps just means it drags on forever."
6. **Carolina:** "Not really. If we do the simple parts first we can actually get something done early. Keep going, {demboyCountry}."
7. **Dem-Boy:** "So like, the first few months is the basics, and then the harder stuff comes later once people trust it."
8. **Power:** "That is a nice idea but it is not realistic."
9. **Carolina:** "It is realistic if we plan it out. {demboyCountry}'s way lets people see it working before they commit to the hard parts."
10. **Power:** "Okay, fine, but the whole thing still has to be done in a year."
11. **Carolina:** "That works. {demboyCountry} maps out the phases, {boss} keeps the one year deadline."
12. **Dem-Boy:** "Cool. {country} will sketch something out."

## 6. PEOPLE, Carolina-led

1. **Carolina:** "Okay, this whole thing is about helping real people, so {country} wants to talk about who this is for."
2. **Power:** "It is for everyone affected. {country} has that covered."
3. **Carolina:** "That is broad. {demboyCountry}, you previously mentioned something more specific?"
4. **Dem-Boy:** "Yeah, so {country} was thinking we should also try to fix why this is happening in the first place..."

10. **Power:** "Fine, but keep it short. Two sentences max on the cause part."
11. **Carolina:** "Two sentences is enough. {demboyCountry} writes the cause part, {boss} handles the response."
12. **Dem-Boy:** "Thanks. {country} will keep it tight."

4. **Power:** "No, we need one clear message. Not a list of everyone's names."
5. **Dem-Boy:** "Right, one message hits harder than five."
6. **Carolina:** "But if we include everyone more people will actually back us up,"

8. **Dem-Boy:** "{boss} is right, the message matters more than the names."
9. **Carolina:** "Even just mentioning that we worked together on this..."
10. **Power:** "We are wasting time. {country}'s point goes first, everything else comes after."
11. **Dem-Boy:** "That is how it should go. Strong opener, then the details."
12. **Power:** "Done. Let's move on."
